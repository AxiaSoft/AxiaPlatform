How to build a fast website

Speed is not a feature you add at the end; it is the result of three decisions made on day one.

Why speed matters

A visitor who waits three seconds usually does not come back. Speed is not a

feature bolted on at the end of a project; it is the result of decisions taken

from the very first day.

The three decisions that matter most

  • Size your images correctly. A 4 MB image displayed in a 400-pixel frame does not just waste bandwidth — it makes the page feel broken.
  • Keep the font light. One variable family with a limited weight range beats four separate files.
  • Defer your JavaScript. Work that is not needed for the first paint must not hold up the first paint.

Measure, do not guess

Before optimising anything, get a number. If you do not know how long the page

currently takes, you cannot say whether the change you made was better or worse.

In summary

Speed is decided in the structure of a project from the start. The three

decisions above make the largest difference with no extra tooling at all.
